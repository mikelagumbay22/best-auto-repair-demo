Our Shop

Best Auto Repair Service is an independent, owner-operated auto repair shop at 3635 Hawkestone Road in Mississauga's Mavis-Erindale area. We've been an incorporated Ontario business since 2008, and we've been looking after local drivers since at least 2014.

Honest Work, Plain Talk

Our customers keep coming back for one reason: we're straight with them. We fix what needs fixing, explain it in plain language and don't push work you don't need. Many of our customers know the owner, Nirmal, by name.
